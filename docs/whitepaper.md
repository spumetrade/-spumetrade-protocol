# Spumetrade Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

**Vision.** Trading on Solana should feel like holding one balance, not juggling a drawer
of tokens. When every price, volume and liquidity figure is read in dollars, comparing
opportunities becomes a matter of judgement instead of arithmetic.

**Mission.** Build a calm, honest front door to Solana markets: show what is moving, keep
custody with the user, and publish every term of the token before anyone is asked to
believe in it.

Principles:

1. Read-only until proven otherwise. Nothing on the site holds funds or requests a signature.
2. One source of truth for the contract address: the bar pinned to the top of the site.
3. No claims about price, yield or returns.
4. Terms are published before they are promoted.

## 3. Roadmap

Phases ship independently. Nothing here is a commitment ahead of release.

### Phase 1: The board

A live market view for Solana: top boosted tokens, price, daily move, volume, liquidity,
a ticker, and a pinned row for the Spumetrade token once its address is public.

### Phase 2: The stash

A single dollar-denominated figure for buying power, so positions and opportunities are
read in one unit.

### Phase 3: The routes

Execution paths that turn a dollar figure into a position, with signing kept on the
user's side.

### Phase 4: The crowd

Community tools, rewards and governance shaped by holders.
